# how you used AI
## Which tools I used, and why it was good

I used ChatGPT. It played three roles in this essay.

First, I used it for -`structure`. I outlined my initial concept to it and asked for help structuring the article's sections. The framework it provided served as an excellent reference: first describing the problem, then explaining why the proposed solution wouldn't work, and finally presenting a better approach. This helped me clarify the specific purpose of each section, making the overall objective of the article much clearer.

Second, I used it for -`titles`. I asked for several title options. which made it easier to write a title closer to my own voice.

Third, I used it for -`translation`. I wrote part of the essay in Chinese first, then asked ChatGPT to translate that section into English.  I also asked it a purely technical question: how to add emphasis and headings in Markdown format.

## One thing it produced that I rejected

I rejected the outline it gave me for the third section.

For that section, I wanted to lay out the final process of generating a dynamic poster in a short, step-by-step form, so that my writing would have a clearer line of thought. I described my actual steps and asked ChatGPT to condense them into a brief list. Instead, it added steps I had never taken: extra tools, extra intermediate stages, and numbers and data points that did not come from my own experience. The list looked more complete and more professional than what I had actually done, but it was not what I had actually done.

So, I discarded that version and wrote this content myself.
